OUR REASON

We're not just a gym.
We're a send club.

Summit City Climbing Co. exists to create a welcoming space where climbing builds connection, confidence, and community impact — on and off the wall.

01

Community

The Fort Wayne climbing scene started as a Facebook group of 550. Now it has a home. Strangers spot each other, beta gets shared, and the floor stays loud.

02

Challenge

USA Climbing certified setters from Chicago, Indy, and Ohio rotate routes every two weeks — V0 to V8 — so the wall is never the same gym twice.

03

Confidence

Bouldering is the doorway. No ropes, no harness — just you, the wall, and the next move. Beginners welcome. GOAT ambassadors on shift.

HOW IT STARTED

Two friends. A two-hour drive.
One stupid good idea.

Edwin Chow (Huntington U) and Collin Jester (IUPUI) discovered climbing in college. The catch — back in Fort Wayne, the closest gym was Indianapolis. Two hours each way. Multiple times a week.

"Every single time we drove down, we're like, 'Dude, we need a climbing gym in Fort Wayne.' Building one was cheaper than the gas." — Edwin Chow, Co-founder

A 2019 attempt by another group fizzled out. Too big. Too expensive. So Edwin and Collin scrapped the multi-million dollar dream and built a small, scrappy proof. 2,130 square feet. One wall. One community.

Today, that proof is open. The route setters drive in from Chicago. The members drive in from Bluffton, Auburn, Decatur, Warsaw. The Facebook group is bigger than ever.

QUESTIONS, PROBABLY

FAQ — but make it real.

I've literally never climbed. Can I come?

Absolutely. Most of our first-timers haven't either. Day pass + rental shoes is $21 total. Tell the front desk it's your first time and a GOAT will walk you through falling, the holds, and basic technique. About 30 minutes in, you'll be hooked.

Do I need a partner / a belayer?

Nope. Bouldering means no ropes, no harness — just walls under 15 ft with crash pads under you. Come solo, come with friends. (When BASECAMP 2 opens, we'll add top-rope.)

What should I wear?

Comfy clothes you can move in. Athletic shorts/leggings + a tee is perfect. Skip jewelry. We rent shoes for $4 — better than your sneakers.

How young is too young?

Kids 5 & under climb free with a paying adult. We have weekly youth programs for ages 6–17. Birthday party minimum is age 5.

I'm a teacher / nurse / first responder / nonprofit lead. Discount?

Yes. Hero pricing is $55/mo (vs $75) for students, teachers, military/veterans, nurses, first responders, social workers, and nonprofit leaders. Show ID at the desk.

Do you sell day passes online?

You can reserve here — payment happens at the front desk. Walk-ins always welcome too.

What's BASECAMP 2?

Our second location, in development. Top-rope, lead, and a way bigger footprint. Sign up for the newsletter to be first in line.
